I built Social Journey Scout with Solari for the Pinetree Research SWE intern challenge.

It turns a public social media journey into a strategy brief:
- Solari browser collects public profile/post evidence
- Solari sandbox scores the narrative arc and content pillars
- the app outputs a brief, evidence JSON, dashboard, and post drafts

Repo: https://github.com/drewmanley16/solari-cookbook
Example: https://github.com/drewmanley16/solari-cookbook/tree/main/examples/social-journey-scout-ts

Subject lens: a builder turning public proof into a social media journey
Sources tested: x.com, www.linkedin.com
