I built Creator Growth Dashboard with Solari for the Pinetree Research SWE intern challenge.

It turns a public social media journey into creator growth assets:
- Solari browser collects public profile/post evidence
- Solari sandbox scores the narrative arc, proof, and content pillars
- the app outputs a content calendar, scripts, partnership outreach, evidence JSON, and a dashboard

Repo: https://github.com/drewmanley16/solari-cookbook
Example: https://github.com/drewmanley16/solari-cookbook/tree/main/examples/creator-growth-dashboard-ts

Subject lens: a builder turning public proof into a social media journey
